# A Novel Geometric Approach to Calculate the Area of a Circle Without the Explicit Use of Pi (π)

## Description
This paper introduces a novel and practical geometric alternative to the traditional formula for calculating the area of a circle (Area = pi * r^2). By analyzing the core geometric relationships between a circle, its inscribed square, and its circumscribed square, a precise algebraic derivation is presented without the direct application of the irrational number pi. The study successfully deduces that the area of a circle can be independently evaluated as 11/14 of the area of its circumscribed square, providing an elegant rational approximation framework that independently validates classical historical approximations.
